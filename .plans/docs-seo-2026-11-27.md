### 2.2 Formatting and voice rules

1. **US English**: `color`, `analyzer`, `license`, `summarize`, `memoize`.
2. **Binary units with space**: `KiB`, `MiB`, `GiB` (e.g. `512 MiB`, `2.4 GiB`, `64 KiB`). Exception: bare `s` (`30s`) or `ms` with space (`22 ms`), and exact CLI filter tokens (`>100MiB`) or upstream config literals (`cacheMaxSize: 1GB`).
3. **Keyboard shortcuts**: `Ctrl+C`, `Ctrl+K`, `Ctrl+O`, `Ctrl+Enter`, `Shift+U`, `Esc`, `Enter`, `Space`, `Tab`, `Backspace`.
4. **Punctuation**: No em dashes (`—`) or spaced hyphens (`-`) in prose. Use colons, commas, or periods.
5. **Headings**: Sentence case (`Configure Sweep`, `Checks before removal`, not `Configure Sweep Options`).
6. **Voice**: Direct address (`you`), active voice, imperative steps, 2–4 sentences per paragraph, no banned filler (`easy`, `simple`, `quick`, `just`, `very`, `really`).
7. **Changeset style**: 1–3 user-focused sentences (what changed for the person running `sweep`, followed by any flag or config name). Move internal implementation details (`DT_VERNEED`, `PATHEXT`, React transforms) to PR descriptions or `.docs/`.
